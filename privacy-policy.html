Privacy Policy for Overflow 2048

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Overflow 2048 ("the Game", "we") handles. We built the Game to collect as little information as possible.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

The Game stores your progress (score, settings, daily streak and missions) on your device. If cloud saving or leaderboards are enabled in a future version, an anonymous player identifier and your score may be stored with Unity Gaming Services so your progress and leaderboard rank can be restored.

Advertising

The Game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect and use device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address, and information about your interactions with ads, in order to show ads, measure performance, prevent fraud and, where you consent, personalize ads. You can learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the Game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy Options in the Game. You can also reset or opt out of the advertising ID in your Android device settings.

In-app purchases

Purchases (for example removing ads or a VIP subscription) are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details.

Children's privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Data processed by Google AdMob and Google Play is governed by Google's own privacy policies. Locally stored game data stays on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the Game.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you. Because we do not collect personal data ourselves, this mostly concerns data held by the advertising and platform providers named above; please contact them directly, or contact us using the details below.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
